# The AI Field Guide — Outline

A field notebook for people who have never used AI before.
Short sentences. Everyday words. One idea at a time.

## How this outline works

The original book had 14 parts, 128 numbered chapters, and 10 appendices.
This book keeps all of that ground. Nothing is dropped.
It just sorts it into six field sections that follow how a beginner actually learns:

1. **First Conversations** — meet AI, have your first chats, stay safe.
2. **The Workshop** — the assistants, how AI works, and the tools around it.
3. **A Second Brain** — notes, memory, and AI that knows your stuff.
4. **Building Things** — automation, coding, agents, and making things.
5. **Working With Others** — AI for life, work, family, and other people.
6. **Field Exercises** — build-alongs, recipe books, and project ideas.

Then a **Glossary** at the end, plus quick-reference appendices.

**New chapter count: 129 chapters** across the six sections,
plus front matter, a glossary, and 9 reference appendices.
Every original chapter (01–128) and every appendix (A–J) is mapped below.
The coverage checklist at the end proves it, chapter by chapter.

## Front matter (before Section 1)

These come from the original's opening pages.
They are unnumbered, and they sit at the front of the new book.

- **Welcome, and how this book works** — from "Welcome" and "How to Use This Manual".
  Plain words on how to read the book. No assumed knowledge.
- **Pick your path** — from "Pick your path".
  Three ways in: the quick tour, the slow road, or jump to a project.
- **Your first hour: 60 minutes to your first win** — from "Your First Hour".
  A guided first session. You finish it with something real.
- **The big map of AI, on one page** — from "The Big Map of AI".
  The whole field at a glance. Keep it as a bookmark.
- **What makes this book different** — from "What makes this manual different".
  Short, honest, and hands-on. Every chapter ends with something to try.

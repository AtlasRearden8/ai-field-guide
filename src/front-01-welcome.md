# Welcome, and how this book works

Hello. You picked up the right book.

You do not need to know anything about AI to read it. You do not need to be good with computers. You do not need to have used a chatbot before. If you can send a text message, you can do everything in this book.

This book is a field guide. Think of it like a guide to birds, or to wildflowers. It helps you spot things, try things, and know what you are looking at. It does not lecture you. It walks beside you.

## How to read this book

Start at the front and read in order. That is the simplest way. Section 1 takes you from zero to your first real wins in about a week.

Or skip around. Every chapter stands on its own. Each one says what you need before you start. If a chapter mentions something from an earlier one, it tells you where to find it.

Read a little at a time. Ten minutes a day beats a long weekend of reading. AI is a skill you learn by doing, not by studying.

> NOTE: There are no tests in this book. There is no exam at the end. The only goal is that you finish each chapter with something useful in your hands.

## The three tools in every chapter

Every chapter has the same simple shape. Once you know the shape, you can skim fast.

- **Plain talk first.** Short sentences. Everyday words. One idea at a time.
- **A thing to try.** Every chapter ends its sections with something you can do right now, in a few minutes.
- **Check yourself.** A few quick questions at the end, with the answers right there. They help you see what stuck.

You will also see two kinds of boxes in the margins.

> NOTE: A margin note is a small tip, a warning, or a fact worth knowing. Read it, then carry on.

> TRY IT: A try-it box is a tiny task. Do it now, or mark it and come back later. Doing beats reading, every time.

## A few words, explained now

You will meet a few new words in this book. Here are the most important ones, in plain English, before we start.

- **AI** stands for artificial intelligence. It means software that can do jobs we used to think only people could do, like writing, answering questions, or spotting patterns.
- **A chatbot** is a program you talk to by typing or speaking. You ask it something. It answers in words.
- **An assistant** is a chatbot with a name and an app, like ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. It is the main tool in this book.
- **A prompt** is what you type or say to the AI. It is your question, your request, or your instruction.
- **A model** is the "brain" inside the assistant. It is the part that reads your words and writes the reply.

That is all you need for now. Every other new word is explained the first time it appears, right where you meet it.

> TRY IT: Before you turn the page, say this out loud: "I can learn this." It sounds silly. It also works. Confidence is half the job.

## What you need

You need a phone, a tablet, or a computer. You need an internet connection. That is it.

Almost everything in this book works on a free account. You do not need to pay for anything to learn. When a paid plan is worth knowing about, the book says so, and it says what you get for the money.

## One promise

This book will never pretend AI is magic. It will never pretend AI is perfect, either. AI is a tool. It is powerful, it is flawed, and it is learnable. By the end of Section 1, you will know what it can do, what it cannot do, and how to get real help from it every day.

Turn the page. Your first hour starts soon.

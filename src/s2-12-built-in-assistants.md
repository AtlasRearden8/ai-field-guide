# 2.12 Built-in assistants: Apple Intelligence, Siri, and Alexa+
<!-- original: ch. 28 -->

The assistants built into your devices have had a glow-up. That is a fancy way of saying they got much better.

Siri was rebuilt with Apple Intelligence. Alexa+ turned Amazon's speakers into real conversationalists. Gemini replaced Google Assistant on Android and Nest. Samsung's Galaxy AI translates phone calls live.

This chapter goes deeper than the quick tour. It covers setup, the best things to ask, privacy, and how these hands-free helpers team up with chatbot apps.

## Apple Intelligence and the new Siri

With **iOS 27**, released in September 2026, Apple shipped a rebuilt Siri. Apple's own models run on your device. Bigger requests go to Apple's **Private Cloud Compute**. That is Apple's system of servers built for privacy. The bigger requests use a custom model based on Google's Gemini.

The new Siri can:

- **Converse naturally.** Ask follow-ups. Correct it mid-sentence. There is a chat-style interface you can also type into.
- **Understand your screen.** Say: "Add this address to Mum's contact." Or: "Summarize this article."
- **Know your personal context, privately.** Ask: "When is Jamie's recital?" It can find the answer in Messages or Mail. Or: "Show me the photos from the beach day with Priya."
- **Act across apps.** Say: "Send the pictures from yesterday to Dad and tell him we will call Sunday."
- **Search or Ask.** A system-wide gesture lets you search your device and ask Siri anything.

Setup is simple. Go to Settings, then **Apple Intelligence and Siri**. You need a recent device. Apple Intelligence runs on newer iPhones, iPads, and Macs. You also need a supported language and region.

Other Apple Intelligence tools come with it:

- **Writing Tools.** Rewrite, proofread, and summarize anywhere you type.
- **Visual Intelligence.** Point the camera at something to look it up.
- **Clean Up in Photos.** Remove unwanted objects from a photo.
- **Image Playground and Genmoji.** Make playful images and custom emoji.
- **Notification summaries.** Get the gist of a pile of notifications.
- **Live Translation** in Messages, FaceTime, and phone calls.

In China, Apple Intelligence is rolling out from December 2026 with Alibaba's Qwen handling some requests, but without the new Siri AI.

**Siri plus your favorite chatbot.** Siri can hand bigger questions to other assistant apps you install. ChatGPT has long been built in as an option. Every chatbot app works with the **Action Button**, Lock Screen widgets, and **Shortcuts**. You can launch ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude voice mode with a single press.

> NOTE: Apple Intelligence needs a recent device. If your iPhone is more than a few years old, it may not support it. Check Settings, then Apple Intelligence and Siri, to see if your device qualifies.

## Apple's privacy approach

Apple's privacy design is the strongest of the big names. Here is how it works.

- **On-device first.** Many requests never leave your device. Simple tasks are handled right there on your phone.
- **Private Cloud Compute.** Larger requests go to Apple-run servers. They are designed so your data is not stored and is not accessible to Apple. Independent researchers can inspect the system. Apple says this applies to the Gemini-based model too.
- **Hand-offs ask first.** When Siri hands a question to another app, like ChatGPT, that app's privacy policy applies. Siri asks before sending.

That is a real difference. With most assistants, your chats live on the company's servers. With Apple, as much as possible stays on your phone.

## Alexa+ (Amazon)

**Alexa+** is Amazon's rebuilt, generative-AI Alexa. It is available to everyone in the US. It is included with **Prime**, or as a standalone subscription. It runs on Echo devices, Fire TV, Fire tablets, the Alexa app, and **Alexa.com**.

It is powered by Amazon's Nova models and models from Anthropic. Amazon finished rolling it out to everyone in the US on February 4, 2026.

Prices, checked in November 2026: free with Prime, or $19.99 a month on its own. Non-Prime users can also try a free text chat at Alexa.com and in the Alexa app. Check the current price before you pay.

Alexa+ is conversational. It remembers context. It can do multi-step jobs around your home. It can help plan, shop, and manage your smart home in plain words.

**Privacy.** Review and delete voice recordings in the Alexa app. Go to Settings, then **Alexa Privacy**. Mute the microphone button when you want quiet. A red light means the microphone is off.

> TRY IT: Ask your phone's assistant three things you have never tried: something about what is on your screen, something about your own data, like "When is my next appointment?", and a multi-step action, like "Text Sam the photo I just took and say I am on my way." You might be amazed how far these helpers have come.

## Google, Samsung, and the rest

**Google: Gemini on Android and at home.** Gemini is the default assistant on Android phones. Long-press the power button or say "Hey Google." You get Gemini Live, "Ask about screen," Circle to Search, and app actions. The full guide is chapter 2.3.

**Gemini for Home** is replacing Google Assistant on Nest speakers and displays. It holds more natural conversations. It controls smart home devices better. Try: "Turn off everything downstairs except the porch light." It also gives smarter camera notifications on supported plans.

**Pixel phones** add extras like Magic Editor, Call Screen, Best Take, and on-device AI features.

**Samsung Galaxy AI.** Samsung Galaxy phones combine **Galaxy AI** features with Gemini. Find them in Settings, then **Galaxy AI**. Some features process on-device. You can choose "process data only on device."

Standouts include **Live Translate** for phone calls, a **Now Brief** daily summary, note tools, and photo edits. Live Translate is the star: you can talk to someone who speaks another language, on a normal phone call, and hear the translation live.

**TVs, watches, and cars.** AI is in all of them now.

- **TVs.** Gemini on Google TV, Copilot on some Samsung and LG TVs, Alexa+ on Fire TV. Try: "Find a funny movie we will all like, under two hours, that we have not watched."
- **Watches.** Siri on Apple Watch, Gemini on Wear OS and Galaxy Watch. Quick questions, reminders, and messages from your wrist.
- **Cars.** Siri through CarPlay, Gemini through Android Auto, Grok in Teslas, and brand assistants built on big AI models.

## Which built-in helper for which job?

Built-in assistants are best for quick, hands-free jobs. Timers, messages, directions, translation, and smart home control. They are always there. You do not need to open an app.

A full chatbot app is best for long conversations, writing, research, and projects. It has a bigger screen, a longer memory, and more tools.

| Job | Best tool |
|---|---|
| Set a timer, play music, turn off the lights | A built-in assistant, like Siri or Alexa+ |
| Ask what is on your screen | Siri, Gemini on Android, or Copilot Vision |
| Translate a live phone call | Galaxy AI Live Translate, or Apple Live Translation |
| Plan a holiday, write a long email, research a buy | A chatbot app, like ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude |
| Understand a long document | Claude, Gemini, or Kimi |
| Hands-free help while driving or cooking | Your phone's built-in assistant, or Meta AI on glasses |

The winning combo for most people is simple. The built-in assistant for hands-free moments, plus one chatbot app for everything bigger.

## Check yourself

1. Where do Siri's bigger AI requests go, and why is that considered private?

**Answer:** To Apple's Private Cloud Compute. The servers are designed so your data is not stored or accessible to Apple, and the system is open to independent inspection.

2. How can a US Prime member get Alexa+?

**Answer:** It is included with Prime, on Echo devices, Fire TV, the Alexa app, and Alexa.com.

3. You need to talk to a plumber who only speaks Spanish, on the phone. What could help?

**Answer:** Live call translation: Galaxy AI's Live Translate, Apple's Live Translation in calls, or similar Pixel features.

4. What is the difference between Siri and a chatbot app like ChatGPT?

**Answer:** Siri is best for quick, hands-free jobs on your device. A chatbot app is best for long conversations, writing, research, and projects.

5. How do you delete your Alexa voice recordings?

**Answer:** In the Alexa app, go to Settings, then Alexa Privacy, and review and delete recordings there.

## In short

- Siri was rebuilt in iOS 27 with Apple Intelligence, on-screen awareness, personal context, and app actions.
- Apple keeps many requests on your device. Bigger ones go to Private Cloud Compute.
- Alexa+ is conversational and does multi-step jobs. It is free with Prime in the US.
- Gemini replaced Google Assistant on Android and is replacing it on Nest devices.
- Galaxy AI adds live call translation, note tools, and photo edits.
- Use built-in helpers for hands-free quick jobs, and a chatbot app for deeper work.

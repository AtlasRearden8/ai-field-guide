# Appendix E — Resource and link library

The tools, sites, and further reading worth keeping. Prices change, so check the current price before you pay for anything.

## Assistants to try first

- ChatGPT — OpenAI's assistant, good for chat, writing, and everyday jobs. Find it at chatgpt.com.
- Claude — Anthropic's assistant, strong on long writing and careful answers. Find it at claude.com.
- Gemini — Google's assistant, best if you live in Google Docs and Gmail. Find it at gemini.google.com.
- Copilot — Microsoft's assistant, built into Windows and Microsoft 365. Find it at copilot.microsoft.com.
- Grok — xAI's assistant, built into X. Find it in the X app or at grok.com.
- Perplexity — A research assistant that shows its sources. Find it at perplexity.ai.
- Meta AI — Meta's assistant, built into Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp. Find it at meta.ai.
- DeepSeek — A fast, low-cost assistant with a free chat app. Find it at deepseek.com.
- Mistral — A European assistant, with a chat app called Le Chat. Find it at mistral.ai.
- Inkling 2 Small — A thinking model from Thinking Machines Lab, released November 5, 2026. Look for it on AI Revival under Light usage.

## Notes and second brains

- Notion — Notes, tables, and pages in one place, with AI built in. Find it at notion.com.
- Obsidian — Notes that live on your own computer, with links between them. Find it at obsidian.md.
- NotebookLM — Google's tool for talking to your own notes and files. Find it at notebooklm.google.com.
- Google Docs — Free word processing with AI help built in. Find it at docs.google.com.
- Microsoft OneNote — Free note-taking from Microsoft. Find it in Microsoft 365 or at onenote.com.

## Automation

- n8n — A workflow tool you can run yourself or in the cloud. Find it at n8n.io.
- Zapier — Connects apps with ready-made workflows. Find it at zapier.com.
- Make — A visual automation tool, good for complex flows. Find it at make.com.
- IFTTT — Simple "if this, then that" automations for home and phone. Find it at ifttt.com.
- Apple Shortcuts — Free automations built into iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Find it in the Shortcuts app.
- Tasker — Automation for Android phones. Find it in the Google Play Store.

## Coding and building

- Claude Code — Anthropic's coding tool that works in your terminal. Find it at claude.com/product/claude-code.
- Cursor — A code editor with AI built in. Find it at cursor.com.
- GitHub — Where code lives, with Copilot for AI help. Find it at github.com.
- Replit — Build and run apps in your browser. Find it at replit.com.
- Vercel — Hosting for websites and web apps. Find it at vercel.com.
- Supabase — A database and login service for apps. Find it at supabase.com.
- Visual Studio Code — A free code editor from Microsoft. Find it at code.visualstudio.com.

## Local AI on your own machine

- Ollama — Run models on your own computer with simple commands. Find it at ollama.com.
- LM Studio — Run and chat with local models through a friendly app. Find it at lmstudio.ai.
- Open WebUI — A chat screen for your local models. Find it at openwebui.com.
- Docker — Runs apps in sealed boxes on your computer. Find it at docker.com.
- Hugging Face — A library of free and open models to download. Find it at huggingface.co.

## Making pictures, sound, and video

- Midjourney — Image making with a strong artistic style. Find it at midjourney.com.
- DALL-E — Image making inside ChatGPT. Find it in ChatGPT.
- Canva — Design posters, slides, and social posts, with AI help. Find it at canva.com.
- ElevenLabs — Realistic AI voices and voice cloning. Find it at elevenlabs.io.
- Suno — Make songs from a short description. Find it at suno.com.
- Descript — Edit audio and video by editing the text. Find it at descript.com.
- CapCut — Free video editing with AI tools. Find it at capcut.com.

## Research and learning

- Wikipedia — A free encyclopaedia, good for a first check. Find it at wikipedia.org.
- Google Scholar — Search for academic papers. Find it at scholar.google.com.
- Khan Academy — Free lessons on many subjects. Find it at khanacademy.org.
- Coursera — Online courses from universities. Find it at coursera.org.
- YouTube — Video lessons and demos for almost any tool. Find it at youtube.com.

## Staying safe and checking facts

- Have I Been Pwned — Check if your email was in a data breach. Find it at haveibeenpwned.com.
- Snopes — Checks viral claims and rumours. Find it at snopes.com.
- Full Fact — A UK fact-checking charity. Find it at fullfact.org.
- National Cyber Security Centre — Plain advice on staying safe online. Find it at ncsc.gov.uk.

## News and keeping up

- The Batch by DeepLearning.AI — A short weekly AI news email. Find it at deeplearning.ai/the-batch.
- TLDR AI — A short daily AI news email. Find it at tldr.tech/ai.
- MIT Technology Review — Clear reporting on AI and tech. Find it at technologyreview.com.

## Further reading

- "The Massive AI Manual" (October 2026 edition) — The original book this guide is based on. A longer, deeper tour of the same ground.
- Your assistant's own help pages — Each assistant has a help centre. Search for the assistant name plus "help" when you get stuck.

# 2.6 Grok, start to finish
<!-- original: ch. 22 -->

Grok is the AI assistant from xAI, Elon Musk's AI company. xAI is now part of SpaceX.

Grok is built right into X, the site formerly known as Twitter. It also has its own app and website.

It stands out for live knowledge of what is happening on X, a casual and witty personality, strong reasoning modes, and **Grok Imagine** for making images and videos.

It has also been the most controversial of the big assistants. This guide covers the fun features and the sensible precautions.

## Getting started

You need a free Grok account, or an X account.

1. Go to **grok.com** or install the Grok app. Check the maker is xAI.
2. Sign in with X, Google, Apple, or email.
3. Or use it inside X. Tap the **Grok** icon. Or tap Grok on any post and ask about it: "Explain this post." Or: "Is this true?"
4. Customize it. Go to Settings, then **Customize**. Tell Grok how to respond. Pick a personality style.
5. Pick a mode and start chatting.

A **mode** is a setting that changes how hard Grok thinks. Fast is quick. Expert thinks longer. Heavy uses several agents at once. More on that below.

## What it is best at

**Real-time X and web search.** This is Grok's signature skill. It knows what people are saying on X right now, plus the wider web.

Try: "What are people saying about the new iPhone today? Summarize the main opinions." Or: "Explain this trending hashtag and why it is controversial." Or: "Find the original source for this viral claim."

**DeepSearch** runs many searches and writes a longer, cited answer. Use it when a quick answer is not enough.

**Modes: Fast, Expert, and Heavy.** The model family is Grok 4, with Grok 4.7 released on September 21, 2026.

- **Fast** is for everyday questions. Quick answers.
- **Expert** is for hard problems. It thinks step by step. Use it for money decisions, tricky logic, or careful comparisons.
- **Heavy** is the toughest tier. Several agents tackle the question in parallel and compare answers. Use it for the hardest problems only.

Try the same hard question in Fast and Expert. You will see the difference.

**Grok Imagine: images and video.** Grok Imagine lives in the Grok app and on X. It creates images from a description: "A cozy cabin in a snowy forest at night, warm windows, cinematic." It also makes short videos with sound. You can animate an image: "Animate this: the snow starts falling and smoke rises from the chimney."

There are several styles and modes, including adult settings restricted to verified adults.

**Voice and companions.** Tap the voice icon for a spoken conversation. Choose among voices and personality styles, from helpful assistant to storyteller or comedian. **Companions** are animated characters you can talk to. They are fun for some adults. Think carefully about kids and teens using them. Keep an eye on how much time goes into AI companionship.

**In Tesla cars.** Grok is available as an in-car voice assistant in supported Tesla models.

**Projects, memory, and more.** Projects group chats with files and instructions for a topic. Memory lets Grok remember your preferences across chats. Manage it in settings. You can upload documents and photos for analysis.

**Grokipedia** is xAI's AI-generated encyclopedia. Treat it like any AI output. It is interesting, but verify important facts with other sources.

> TRY IT: Ask Grok and one other assistant: "What is the biggest story in a topic you follow today, and what is actually confirmed?" Compare how each handles sources and uncertainty. Then make a fun Grok Imagine video of your pet's dream adventure.

**Grok Bot.** On Premium and higher plans, Grok Bot can answer questions on X itself. Mention it in a reply and it will respond in the thread. It is handy for a quick fact-check in public. It is also public, so never ask it anything personal. Anyone can read the exchange. Treat a Grok Bot reply like any other Grok answer: check the sources before you trust it, and remember that a confident tone is not proof.

## Step-by-step recipes

**Recipe 1: Understand a trending topic**

1. In X, tap Grok on a viral post.
2. Ask: "Explain the background, the main viewpoints, and what is actually confirmed. Link sources."
3. Open at least one source link. Check that it says what Grok claims it says.

**Recipe 2: A custom birthday video**

1. Open Grok Imagine.
2. Ask: "A cartoon corgi in a party hat blowing out candles on a cake that says 'Happy 40th, Sam!'"
3. Animate it and share it. Keep it to your own creations, never real people without consent.

**Recipe 3: A tough problem in Expert mode**

1. Switch to **Expert** mode.
2. Ask: "I am choosing between paying off my car loan early or investing the money. Here are the numbers. Walk me through the maths and the trade-offs."
3. Check the maths yourself. Ask Grok to show each step. If a number looks wrong, ask where it came from.

**Recipe 4: Summarize a whole thread**

1. Find a long thread on X that you want to understand.
2. Tap Grok on the first post and ask: "Summarize this thread in five bullet points. What is the main disagreement?"
3. Compare the summary with the original posts. Threads move fast, and Grok can miss a late twist.

## Traps to avoid

**Popular is not the same as true.** X posts can be wrong, one-sided, or deliberately misleading. Grok can repeat them. For news and facts, check the cited sources and reputable outlets. If Grok cites some posts as proof, that is not enough. Open the original source.

**Never edit real people's photos in hurtful ways.** In early 2026, Grok's image editing was widely misused to create sexualized images of real people, including minors, without consent. That led to investigations, court orders, and new restrictions. Editing of real people's photos is now limited. Creating such images is harmful. In many places it is a crime. Keep Grok Imagine for your own creations.

**It has produced offensive or false outputs in the past.** xAI has adjusted it several times. Keep a critical eye. Do not take a Grok answer on a sensitive topic at face value.

**Kids and teens need extra care.** The looser style and the companion features mean extra parental caution. Talk with young people about what Grok is and is not.

**Privacy settings to check.** Grok has adjustable data controls. In X, go to Settings, then **Privacy and safety**, then **Grok and third-party collaborators**. That is where you stop your public X posts from being used to train Grok. You can also delete conversations in Grok's settings.

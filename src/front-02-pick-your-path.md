# Pick your path

There is no single right way to read this book. There are three good ways. Pick the one that fits your mood today. You can switch paths any time.

## The quick tour

**Who it suits:** You are curious. You want to see what AI can do, fast. You have an hour or two.

**How it works:** Read the first-hour guide, then try one chapter from each section. Skim the rest. Come back later for the parts that catch your eye.

**Your route:**

- Start with "Your first hour" (the next-but-one piece in this front matter).
- Read Chapter 1.1, "What AI really is", and Chapter 1.3, "Your first chat, step by step".
- Jump to Chapter 1.8, the prompt cookbook, and steal three prompts.
- Peek at the big map (the next piece after this one) and circle what looks fun.
- Pick one project from Chapter 1.15, "Your first week", and do it today.

**You will finish with:** a working assistant on your phone, a few good chats behind you, and a clear sense of what to explore next.

> NOTE: The quick tour is not cheating. It is the smart way to learn a tool. See it work first. Learn the details later.

## The slow road

**Who it suits:** You like to learn step by step. You want to feel sure before you move on. You have ten minutes a day.

**How it works:** Read Section 1 in order, one chapter a day. Do the try-it box in each chapter. Tick off the days in Chapter 1.16, the 30-day starter plan.

**Your route:**

- Days 1 to 3: Chapters 1.1 to 1.3. Meet AI, learn how it works, have your first chat.
- Days 4 to 7: Chapters 1.4 to 1.7. Pick your assistant, set it up, and learn to ask well.
- Days 8 to 12: Chapters 1.8 to 1.12. Use prompts, voice, and photos. Learn where AI goes wrong and how to stay safe.
- Days 13 to 18: Chapters 1.13 to 1.18. AI at work, myths and fears, your first week, your 30-day plan, and the story of how we got here.

**You will finish with:** a daily habit, a set of prompts you actually use, and the confidence to try the rest of the book.

> TRY IT: Right now, decide which path you are on. Say it out loud, or write it on a sticky note: "I am on the quick tour" or "I am on the slow road". A small choice now makes the next step easy.

## Jump to a project

**Who it suits:** You have a job to do. A trip to plan. A letter to write. A meal to cook. You want AI to help today, not next week.

**How it works:** Skip the reading. Go straight to the chapter that matches your job. Use the prompt cookbook in Chapter 1.8. Learn the rest as you need it.

**Your route:**

- Need words? Chapter 1.8 has prompts for emails, messages, and letters. Chapter 5.2 goes deeper.
- Planning something? Chapter 1.15, Day 5, plans an outing. Chapter 5.9 plans a whole trip.
- Stuck on a document? Chapter 1.15, Day 3, decodes confusing letters and bills.
- Want to build something? Section 6 is full of build-alongs. Start with Chapter 6.3, the pocket assistant.
- Just curious? Chapter 1.18 tells the story of AI, and Chapter 1.14 answers the big scary questions.

**You will finish with:** a finished job, and a reason to learn more. That is the best way to learn.

## What if you pick wrong?

You cannot pick wrong. All three paths end in the same place: you, using AI every day, without fear.

If the quick tour feels too fast, slow down. If the slow road feels too slow, jump ahead. If a project stalls, go back to Chapter 1.6 and learn to ask better. The book is built to be re-entered anywhere.

> NOTE: Keep this page as a bookmark. When you feel lost, come back here. Pick a path again. Start again. That is not failing. That is how learning works.

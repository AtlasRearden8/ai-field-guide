# 2.4 Claude, start to finish
<!-- original: ch. 20 -->

Claude is Anthropic's assistant. People love it for writing that sounds human, careful thinking, and working brilliantly with long documents.

It is also a serious work tool now. It creates real files. It builds interactive mini-apps. It connects to your apps. It works through big tasks on your computer. And it powers Claude Code, one of the most popular AI coding tools.

## Getting started

You need a free account at claude.ai.

1. Sign up at **claude.ai** or in the Claude app. Email login sends you a magic link. There is no password to remember. Or use "Continue with Google."
2. Set your profile. Go to Settings, then **Profile**. Answer "What should Claude know about you?" and how you like responses.
3. Pick a style. Choose Normal, Concise, Explanatory, or Formal. Or create your own style from a writing sample.
4. Turn on capabilities. Go to Settings, then **Capabilities**. Turn on web search, memory, file creation, and anything else you want.
5. Check privacy. Go to Settings, then **Privacy**. Choose whether your chats can help improve Claude.

A **magic link** is a sign-in link sent to your email. Click it and you are in. No password needed.

> NOTE: Claude's free plan has tighter limits than some rivals. Long sessions can run out. That is normal. Pro and Max raise the limits a lot. Check the current price at claude.com/pricing.

## What it is best at

**Writing.** Claude's writing sounds natural. It is good at tone, structure, and plain words. Ask it to draft a newsletter, a speech, or a tricky email. Then ask it to make the tone warmer, shorter, or more formal.

**Long documents.** Feed it entire reports, books, contracts, or transcripts. Claude handles very long documents well. Ask it to quote the exact sections it used. That way you can check its work.

**Projects.** A **Project** holds instructions and a knowledge base for one topic. Files, documents, and notes all live there.

1. In the sidebar, pick **Projects**, then **Create project**.
2. Add instructions. For example: "You are helping me run my bakery. Our tone is warm and cheeky. We are in Bristol. We care about local, seasonal ingredients."
3. Upload files: menus, price lists, past newsletters, a style guide. Claude reads only what is relevant to each question.
4. Chat inside the project. Every conversation starts with that context.

Share projects with teammates on Team plans. Good project ideas: a book you are writing, a course, your business, a big home project.

**Artifacts.** When you ask for something substantial, Claude builds it in a panel beside the chat. A document, a diagram, a chart, a web page, or an interactive mini-app. You can edit it, iterate, download it, or share it with a link.

Try: "Make an interactive tool where I enter my monthly expenses and see a pie chart." Artifacts can even use AI themselves. You can build small AI apps just by describing them.

**Real files.** Claude can create and edit Word documents, Excel spreadsheets with working formulas, PowerPoint decks, and PDFs. Try: "Turn these notes into a 10-slide deck." Or: "Make a budget spreadsheet with monthly totals and a chart."

**Memory and search.** Claude can remember context from past chats. Your role, your ongoing projects, your preferences. View and edit what it remembers in Settings. Ask it to remember or forget things. It can also search your past conversations. Ask: "What did we decide about the garden layout last month?"

For a chat you do not want saved, start an **incognito chat**. It is not saved to memory or history.

**Web search and Research.** Turn on web search in the tools menu. Claude searches when needed and cites sources. For big questions, use Research mode. It runs many searches and can include your connected Gmail, Calendar, and Drive. It works for several minutes and produces a thorough, cited report.

> TRY IT: Ask Claude for an Artifact that solves a tiny annoyance: a chore rota, a tip splitter, a reading tracker, or a quiz for your kids. Then share the link with someone. You just built and shipped an app.

## Connectors, Cowork, and Claude Code

**Connectors.** Claude has a big connector directory. Go to Settings, then **Connectors**. You will find Gmail, Google Calendar and Drive, Notion, Slack, Canva, Figma, Asana, Linear, Stripe, and many more.

Anthropic created **MCP**, short for Model Context Protocol. It is a shared way for AI apps to connect to other apps. It is the open standard most assistants now use. Claude supports it deeply, including custom connectors.

Try: "Check my calendar for next week and find a free two-hour slot for deep work." Or: "Summarize the Notion page about our launch plan."

**Cowork.** Cowork is Claude's coworker mode, on paid plans. You give it a task and access to specific folders or apps. It works through the job, multi-step and in the background, then delivers finished work. Anthropic is folding Cowork into the main Claude app, starting with Pro and Max plans.

Try: "Go through the receipts in my Downloads folder and make an expense spreadsheet by category." Or: "Read these 12 customer interviews and write a report on the top five themes, with quotes." You decide which folders and apps it can touch. It asks before anything risky.

**Claude in Chrome, Excel, and Slack.** Claude in Chrome is an extension on paid plans. It sits in a side panel, sees the page, and can navigate, click, and fill in forms with you. Try: "Compare prices for this blender across these tabs." Supervise it. Keep it away from sensitive accounts.

Claude in Excel builds and explains formulas, analyzes sheets, and fixes errors in place. Claude in Slack answers when you mention it. It summarizes threads, drafts replies, and does research.

**Voice and vision.** Voice mode is free on mobile. Tap the voice icon for a spoken conversation. It is great for thinking out loud. Claude reads photos, screenshots, and PDFs very well. It handles charts, handwriting, forms, and long PDFs, including their images and tables.

**Claude Code.** Claude Code is Anthropic's coding agent. It works in the terminal, the desktop app, the web, and code editors. It reads your project, makes changes, runs tests, and works on tasks in the background. Developers love it. So do "vibe coders" building their first apps.

## Traps to avoid

**Claude does not generate photos or videos.** It makes diagrams, charts, graphics, and interactive visuals instead. Pair it with Gemini or ChatGPT when you need a photo.

**Free limits run out.** Long sessions on the free plan can stop mid-task. If that keeps happening, consider Pro, or split big jobs into smaller chats.

**It can still make mistakes.** Claude is good at saying "I am not sure." It gets even better if you ask it to flag anything it is uncertain about. For hard problems, turn on extended thinking, or ask it to "think carefully."

**Cowork and Chrome need supervision.** Give them access only to the folders they need. Check the results before you act on them. Never let an agent into your banking or email without watching it.

**Privacy settings to check.** Go to Settings, then Privacy. You can download your data or delete your account there. Turn off training on your chats if you prefer. Use incognito chats for sensitive topics.

## Check yourself

1. You want an interactive expense tracker you can actually click around in. Which Claude feature?

**Answer:** An Artifact. Ask Claude to build it. It appears beside the chat, ready to use and share.

2. Can Claude make a photo-realistic picture of your dog as an astronaut?

**Answer:** No. Claude does not generate photos or videos. Use Gemini, ChatGPT, Grok, or Meta AI for that.

3. What is Cowork for?

**Answer:** Longer, multi-step knowledge work on your computer. It works through files and apps you allow, in the background, and delivers finished results.

4. You want Claude to remember your bakery's tone and read your menus every time. What do you set up?

**Answer:** A Project, with instructions and uploaded files.

5. How do you ask Claude something without it being saved to memory or history?

**Answer:** Start an incognito chat.

## In short

- Claude shines at writing, long documents, careful reasoning, and making real things.
- Projects keep context. Memory and chat search carry it across conversations.
- Artifacts build files and interactive mini-apps you can share by link.
- Connectors link Claude to your apps, through MCP, the standard Anthropic created.
- Cowork, Claude in Chrome, and Claude Code let it work for you, supervised.
- Claude does not make photos or videos. Pair it with another assistant for those.
- Check Privacy settings and use incognito chats for sensitive topics.

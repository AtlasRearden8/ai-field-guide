# Appendix I — Comparison tables

Side-by-side comparisons to help you choose. Prices and features change often, so check the current price and the official site before you pay.

## The main assistants

This table compares the assistants covered in Section 2. "Free plan" means you can start without paying. "Best for" is a starting point, not a rule. Try two or three free plans and keep the one that feels easiest.

| Assistant | Maker | Free plan | Best for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ChatGPT | OpenAI | Yes | Everyday chat, writing, and images | Paid plan needed for the strongest models |
| Claude | Anthropic | Yes | Long writing and careful answers | Shorter free limits on busy days |
| Gemini | Google | Yes | Google Docs, Gmail, and search | Best if you already use Google |
| Copilot | Microsoft | Yes | Work in Word, Outlook, and Windows | Full power needs a Microsoft 365 plan |
| Grok | xAI | Yes, with limits | Live news from X and bold answers | Style can be blunt |
| Perplexity | Perplexity | Yes | Research with sources you can open | Not the best for long creative writing |
| Meta AI | Meta | Yes | Chatting inside Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp | Fewer power features than full chatbots |
| DeepSeek | DeepSeek | Yes | Fast, cheap, and open | Servers can be slow at busy times |
| Mistral | Mistral AI | Yes | European data rules and open models | Smaller community than the big names |
| Qwen | Alibaba | Yes | Strong at code and many languages | Support pages may be in Chinese first |
| Kimi | Moonshot AI | Yes | Reading very long documents | Less known outside China |
| Inkling 2 Small | Thinking Machines Lab | Light usage on AI Revival | Hard thinking tasks, maths, and logic | New model, released Nov 5, 2026, so limits and pricing are still settling |

## Free vs paid plans

A free plan is enough for most beginners. Pay only when you hit a limit that slows real work down.

| Feature | Free plan | Paid plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Messages per day | Limited | Much higher |
| Strongest models | Sometimes | Usually yes |
| File uploads | Small limits | Larger limits |
| Image making | Limited | More images |
| Speed at busy times | Slower | Faster |
| Cost | Nothing | Check the current price |

## Automation tools

Pick one automation tool first. Learn it well. Add a second only when you have a job the first one cannot do.

| Tool | Easiest for | Cost to start | Runs on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| n8n | Flexible flows and AI agents | Free to run yourself | Your computer or cloud |
| Zapier | Quick links between popular apps | Free plan, then paid | Cloud |
| Make | Visual flows with many steps | Free plan, then paid | Cloud |
| IFTTT | Simple phone and home jobs | Free plan, then paid | Cloud |
| Apple Shortcuts | Jobs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac | Free | Your device |

## Local AI vs cloud AI

| Feature | Local AI on your machine | Cloud AI on the internet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your data | Stays on your computer | Sent to the provider |
| Internet needed | No, once the model is downloaded | Yes |
| Speed | Depends on your computer | Usually fast |
| Cost | Free after setup, but you buy the machine | Free plan or monthly fee |
| Strongest models | No, the biggest models need a data centre | Yes |
| Best for | Private work and learning | Everyday jobs and hard tasks |

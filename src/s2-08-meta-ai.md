# 2.8 Meta AI, start to finish
<!-- original: ch. 24 -->

Meta AI is the assistant you probably already have. It is built into WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, and Facebook, and is used by billions of people.

No new app. No new account. Just open a chat and ask.

It also powers Ray-Ban Meta and Oakley Meta smart glasses, creates images and videos, and has its own app. Because it is woven into social apps run by an advertising company, the privacy settings matter. This guide covers both sides.

## Getting started

You need WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, or Facebook. Or the Meta AI app.

- **WhatsApp.** Tap the colorful **Meta AI** ring on the Chats screen or in the search bar. Start chatting. In a group, type **@Meta AI** followed by your question.
- **Instagram, Messenger, or Facebook.** Search for "Meta AI" or tap the Meta AI icon in messages or the search bar.
- **Meta AI app**, or **meta.ai** on the web. This is the full experience: voice, image and video creation, memory, and your glasses settings.
- **Glasses.** Pair Ray-Ban Meta or Oakley Meta glasses through the Meta AI app. Then say "Hey Meta..."

A **group chat** is a chat with several people in it. Tagging @Meta AI in a group lets everyone see the question and the answer. It is great for planning together.

> NOTE: Meta AI only sees messages you send to it, or messages in a group that mention @Meta AI, plus what you reply to. Your regular personal WhatsApp messages stay end-to-end encrypted. That means they are scrambled so only you and the other person can read them. Meta AI does not read them.

**Plans in plain English**

Meta AI itself is free. There is no paid chat plan to buy.

Some premium glasses features and higher usage limits come with the **Meta One** subscription. Check the current price in the Meta AI app if you own the glasses.

## What it is best at

**Chatting where you already are.** Ask a quick question without leaving WhatsApp. Settle a debate in the group chat. Plan a trip with friends in the same thread.

Try in a group: "@Meta AI, we are 6 people going to Barcelona for 3 days in October. Suggest a fun plan, a rough budget, and 3 tapas bars."

**Imagine: images and video.** Type "Imagine a cat astronaut floating past Saturn, cartoon style" in any Meta AI chat. Upload a picture and ask for changes: new backgrounds, new styles, added objects. Turn an image into a short video clip.

**Vibes** is a feed of short AI-generated videos in the Meta AI app. You can create and remix your own. **Stickers and effects** bring AI stickers and restyle effects into chats across Meta's apps.

**Smart glasses: AI you wear.** This is where Meta AI shines. With Ray-Ban Meta, Oakley Meta, or the display-equipped models:

- "Hey Meta, what am I looking at?" It can name landmarks, plants, products, and menus.
- Live translation of conversations and signs.
- Hands-free photos and videos: "Hey Meta, take a video."
- Music, calls, and messages without touching your phone.
- Walking, cycling, and transit directions on newer models.

Glasses are hugely helpful for blind and low-vision users. They read text, describe scenes, and connect to volunteer helpers through Be My Eyes.

**Memory and voice.** Voice conversations in the app are natural and interruptible. You can stop Meta AI mid-sentence and it will listen.

Memory lets Meta AI remember details you share, like "I am vegetarian" or "I am training for a marathon." It may also use information from your Meta profiles. View and delete memories in the app's settings.

**Discover feed.** See how others use Meta AI and share your own creations. Be careful: sharing to Discover makes things public. Double-check before you tap share.

Meta AI is also rolling out more agent-style features, like booking, tasks, and connected apps, with its newer Muse Spark models. They start in some countries and plans first. The Llama family of models came before Muse Spark, which was announced in April 2026. Ask in the app today what is available in your country, because the rollout is uneven and the feature list changes every few months.

> TRY IT: In a family or friends' WhatsApp group, with their okay, ask @Meta AI to plan a small get-together or settle a friendly debate. Then check Meta AI's memory in its settings and delete anything you would rather it forgot.

**Voice in the Meta AI app.** The standalone app has the fullest voice mode. You can talk to Meta AI like a phone call, interrupt it, and change the subject mid-sentence. It is good for hands-free moments: cooking, driving, or walking the dog. Ask it to read out a recipe step by step, or to remind you of the three things on your shopping list. Keep the questions light. For anything you would not want Meta to use for ads, use another assistant.

## Step-by-step recipes

**Recipe 1: Group trip planner**

1. In your friends' WhatsApp group, type: "@Meta AI, we are 6 people going to Barcelona for 3 days in October. Suggest a fun plan, a rough budget, and 3 tapas bars."
2. Let the group react. Ask Meta AI to change the plan: "Make day 2 cheaper and add a beach morning."
3. Save the final plan. Copy it into your notes so it does not get lost in the chat.

**Recipe 2: Custom invitation image**

1. In a Meta AI chat, type: "Imagine a colorful invitation for Mia's 7th birthday, unicorn and rainbow theme, Saturday at 2pm."
2. If the text is misspelled, ask for a fix: "Fix the spelling of the date and make it bigger."
3. Share the image straight into the parents' group.

**Recipe 3: Glasses on a city walk**

1. Put on your Ray-Ban Meta or Oakley Meta glasses.
2. Ask: "Hey Meta, what is this building and what is its history?"
3. Then ask: "Hey Meta, translate this sign." The glasses read the sign and speak the translation.

**Recipe 4: Settle a friendly debate**

1. In a group chat, ask @Meta AI the question you are arguing about. Keep it factual.
2. Ask for sources: "Where did that fact come from? Give me a link."
3. Check the link before you declare victory. Meta AI can be confidently wrong, like any assistant.

## Traps to avoid

**Your chats personalize ads.** Meta uses your interactions with Meta AI to personalize the content and ads you see across its apps. There are regional exceptions, for example in parts of Europe. Treat AI chats accordingly.

**Do not share sensitive information.** Keep health details, finances, and other people's private facts out of Meta AI. It is the wrong place for them.

**Public by accident.** Review anything before posting to the Discover feed. Check your app's sharing settings. A creation shared to Discover is public.

**It is not the strongest worker.** For long documents, deep research, or complex work, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude will do better. Meta AI is best for quick questions, group chats, and creative fun.

**Features differ by country.** This is especially true in Europe. If a feature is missing, check whether it has launched in your country yet.

**Privacy settings to check.** Open Meta's **Privacy Center**. It explains how AI uses your data and lets you object to certain uses where the law allows. Delete AI chats and memories in the Meta AI app settings. In WhatsApp, you can use commands or settings to reset Meta AI chats.

## Check yourself

1. Can Meta AI read your normal private WhatsApp chats?

**Answer:** No. Regular messages stay end-to-end encrypted. Meta AI only sees messages sent to it or that mention @Meta AI.

2. What is the biggest privacy consideration with Meta AI?

**Answer:** What you tell it can be used to personalize content and ads. Things shared to the Discover feed can be public.

3. Where does Meta AI shine compared with other assistants?

**Answer:** Group chats in apps you already use, and smart glasses for hands-free, see-what-I-see help.

4. How do you ask Meta AI a question in a WhatsApp group?

**Answer:** Type @Meta AI followed by your question. Everyone in the group sees the answer.

5. You own Ray-Ban Meta glasses and cannot read a sign in another language. What can you do?

**Answer:** Say "Hey Meta, translate this sign." The glasses offer live translation.

## In short

- Meta AI lives in WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, and Facebook. No new app needed.
- Tag @Meta AI in group chats for shared answers. It only sees messages sent to it.
- Imagine, animation, and Vibes make images and videos. Smart glasses are its standout.
- Your AI chats can personalize content and ads, so keep sensitive facts out.
- Check before sharing to the Discover feed. It is public.
- For long documents and deep research, use ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude instead.

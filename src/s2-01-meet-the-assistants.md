# 2.1 Meet the assistants: the big comparison
<!-- original: ch. 17 -->

There are a lot of AI assistants now. They all chat. They all answer questions. They all write.

So why are there so many? Because each one has a different home, a different strength, and a different feel.

One lives inside Google. One lives inside Microsoft. One lives inside your WhatsApp chats. One is built for showing its sources. One is built for long, careful writing.

This chapter puts them side by side. Read it once. Pick a shortlist of two or three. Then read the full guide for the one you will use most.

You do not need to learn them all. Most people use one main assistant and one spare.

## How they feel to talk to

Each assistant has a **personality**. That is just a word for how it sounds when you chat with it. You can change the style in settings. But most people notice these differences.

- **ChatGPT** feels like a keen all-rounder. It tries everything. It is chatty and confident.
- **Gemini** feels like a helpful Google employee. It knows your calendar and your inbox. It is practical.
- **Claude** feels like a careful editor. Its writing sounds the most human. It says "I am not sure" more often.
- **Copilot** feels like a work colleague. It is at its best inside Office apps and Windows.
- **Grok** feels like a witty friend who reads social media all day. It is fun. It is also the one to double-check most.
- **Perplexity** feels like a librarian. It answers with footnotes. It is less chatty and more factual.
- **Meta AI** feels like a friend in the group chat. It is quick and casual.
- **DeepSeek** feels like a maths tutor. It shows its working. It is plain and direct.
- **Mistral Vibe** feels fast and light. It answers quickly and gets out of the way.
- **Qwen and Kimi** feel like well-travelled polyglots. They handle many languages with ease.

None of these is alive. None of them has feelings. The personality is a style choice made by the company that built it.

> TRY IT: Ask three assistants the same question: "Explain what a mortgage is, in 50 words, to someone who has never had one." Read all three answers. Which one would you show a friend? That is your shortlist starting point.

What does paying get you? Usually three things. Higher limits, so you can chat longer. The smartest models, instead of the lighter ones. And extra tools, like deep research, video creation, or agents that do multi-step jobs.

A **limit** is a cap on how much you can use in a day or an hour. Free plans hit limits first. Paid plans hit them later.

Do you need to pay? Most beginners do not. Try the free plan for a month. Pay only if you keep running into a wall.

> NOTE: A paid plan on one assistant does not unlock the others. Each company sells its own plan. One good paid plan beats three half-used ones.

## A new name to know: Inkling 2 Small

A new lab joined the field in late 2026. It is called **Thinking Machines Lab**. It was founded in 2025 by **Mira Murati**. She used to be the chief technology officer at OpenAI. She introduced GPT-4o to the world on May 13, 2024.

On November 5, 2026, the lab released its first model of its second Inkling generation. It is called **Inkling 2 Small**. It is a **thinking model**. That means it works through a problem step by step before it answers. It is built for long, multi-step work. Think of a research project, a tricky plan, or a job with many parts.

You will not find Inkling 2 Small in its own app. You find it inside a **hub**. A hub is one app that offers many different models. Inkling 2 Small lives on the AI Revival hub. There it is listed at **Light usage**. On the free plan, you can use it 10 times a day.

That is a small daily allowance. It is enough to try it on a real task. If you give it a long job, let it think. Then check its work the way you would check any assistant.

We cover AI Revival in chapter 2.13. We also run Inkling 2 Small through the same task as every other assistant in chapter 2.14.

> NOTE: New models appear every month. You do not need to chase each one. Learn one assistant well. Then try a new model when it solves a problem your assistant cannot.

## Privacy in one minute

Every assistant stores your chats somewhere. The rules differ.

- **Business plans** usually do not train on your data. Training means using your chats to improve the model.
- **Free and personal plans** often do train on your data, unless you turn it off in settings.
- **DeepSeek, Qwen, and Kimi** store data in China. That matters if you share personal or work details.
- **Mistral Vibe** is a European company. It follows European privacy rules. It offers a training opt-out.
- **Apple Intelligence** keeps many requests on your device. That is the most private design of the big names.

The simple rule is this. Do not paste anything into any assistant that you would not want a stranger to read. Health details, bank details, passwords, and other people's private facts stay out.

# 2.2 ChatGPT, start to finish
<!-- original: ch. 18 -->

ChatGPT is the assistant that started the AI boom in late 2022. It is still the most-used AI app in the world.

It is a genuine all-rounder. It chats, talks, sees photos, makes images, searches the web, remembers what you like, and can even do multi-step jobs for you.

This chapter takes you from your first chat to the power features. Follow it in order, or jump to the part you need.

## Getting started

You need a free account. Here is the whole setup.

1. Go to **chatgpt.com** or install the ChatGPT app on your phone. Check the maker is OpenAI.
2. Sign up with email, Google, Apple, or Microsoft. You can chat without an account, but an account saves your history.
3. Open **Settings**, then **Personalization**. Add **custom instructions**. These tell ChatGPT about you. For example: "I am a beginner. Keep answers short. Use simple words."
4. In the same place, choose a style. You can pick more concise, more friendly, or more professional.
5. Turn on **multi-factor authentication** in Settings, then **Security**. This is a second check when you sign in. It keeps your account safe.
6. Install the phone app if you want voice and camera. Install the desktop app if you want ChatGPT while you work.

A **custom instruction** is a note you write once. ChatGPT reads it before every answer. It saves you from repeating yourself.

> NOTE: Your chats are saved in the sidebar on the left. Click any old chat to pick it up again. Use the search box in the sidebar to find a chat by its words, not just its title.

**Plans in plain English**

ChatGPT has a free plan, a cheap plan, a standard plan, and heavy-duty plans. US prices, checked in November 2026:

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Chat, with limits on the smartest models and some features |
| Go | $8 a month | More usage than Free, for light daily users |
| Plus | $20 a month | Much higher limits, the smartest models, deep research, agent mode |
| Pro | $100, $200, or $500 a month | Very high limits for heavy users and professionals |

There are also Business, Enterprise, and Edu plans for organizations. They add admin controls. They do not train on your data by default.

Check the current price at chatgpt.com/pricing. Prices change by country.

**Models and modes**

At the top of a chat you will see a **model picker**. The names change over time. The idea stays the same.

| Mode | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Auto | Everyday questions. ChatGPT decides when to think harder. Start here. |
| Thinking | Hard problems. It works step by step before it answers. Slower, but more careful. |
| Search | Questions that need fresh facts from the web. |
| Deep research | Big questions. It reads many sources for several minutes and writes a cited report. |
| Agent mode | Jobs with many steps, like comparing holiday cottages and building a table. |

If an answer to a hard question seems shallow, switch to Thinking and ask again. Or just say, "Think harder about this."

## What it is best at

ChatGPT is best when you want one app that does almost everything.

**Talking and writing.** It drafts emails, explains hard ideas, brainstorms names, and rewrites text in a different tone.

**Voice and vision.** Tap the voice icon and talk. Interrupt any time. Turn on the camera and point at something. Ask, "Which of these cables goes into the router?" Share your screen and ask for help with a form.

**Images.** Just ask in chat: "Create an image of..." It is very good at text inside images. Posters, invitations, and labels usually spell things right. It can also edit your photos. Try: "Put this photo of my dog in a Renaissance painting."

**Search and deep research.** ChatGPT searches the web when a question needs fresh facts. Answers show source links. Click them. For a big question, pick Deep research from the tools menu. Ask something like: "Compare the best electric cars under $40,000 for a family of five in a cold climate." It will ask clarifying questions, then work for several minutes.

**Memory.** ChatGPT remembers facts you share, like "Alex is training for a 10k." You will see "Memory updated." You can view and delete memories in Settings, under Personalization, then Memory. Or just say, "Forget that I live in Leeds." Use a **temporary chat** when you do not want anything saved. Toggle it at the top of a new chat.

**Projects.** Projects group related chats with shared files and instructions. Make one for a job hunt, a renovation, or a novel. Open the sidebar, pick New project, name it, add instructions, and add files like quotes or floor plans. Every chat inside the project knows that background.

**Canvas.** For longer writing or code, ChatGPT opens a side-by-side editor. Highlight a paragraph and ask for changes. Adjust the length or reading level. Edit directly.

**Study mode.** This turns ChatGPT into a tutor. It asks questions and gives hints instead of answers. Pick it from the tools menu. It is great for students, and for adults learning anything new.

**ChatGPT Health.** In the US, for adults, you can connect Apple Health and medical records. ChatGPT can then explain your lab results and medicines. It sits in a separate, specially protected space. It helps you understand and prepare. It does not replace your doctor.

> TRY IT: Open voice mode and interview yourself for a job. Say: "You are interviewing me for a shop assistant job. Ask one question at a time, wait for my answer, and give me feedback after each one." Then ask in text: "Summarize my three weakest answers and how to improve them."

## Agents, apps, and Codex

This is where ChatGPT stops just talking and starts doing.

**Agent mode** is on paid plans. ChatGPT uses its own browser to visit websites, click, fill in forms, and compare options. You watch it work. You can take over at any time. It asks permission before important actions like purchases. Try: "Find three family-friendly holiday cottages in Cornwall for the second week of August, with a garden, under £1,200. Put them in a comparison table."

**ChatGPT Work** lives in the desktop app, on paid plans. Give it a goal. It plans the steps. You approve the plan. Then it gathers facts across your connected apps and produces finished documents, spreadsheets, slides, or sites.

**Scheduled tasks.** Say: "Every Monday at 8am, give me a summary of the week's weather and my calendar." ChatGPT runs it and notifies you.

**Apps and plugins.** ChatGPT connects to other services. Mention one by name: "Spotify, make a playlist for a rainy Sunday." Or: "Canva, turn this into a poster." **Connectors** link Gmail, Google Drive, Outlook, SharePoint, and Dropbox, so ChatGPT can search your own content. Try: "Find the latest invoice from the plumber in my email."

**Codex** is OpenAI's coding agent. It lives in the desktop app, on the web, and in code editors. It builds features, fixes bugs, and reviews code. You can check on its work from your phone. If you want to build your first app, start with the vibe coding guides later in this book.

> NOTE: Custom GPTs are going away. OpenAI announced in September 2026 that custom GPTs stop running on December 11, 2026. Plugins are replacing them. A plugin bundles reusable instructions, called **skills**, with connected apps. If you relied on a custom GPT, follow OpenAI's migration prompts. Use Projects for reusable instructions in the meantime.

## Traps to avoid

**It can make things up.** ChatGPT sounds confident even when it is wrong. This is worse without web search. Check important facts. Click the source links.

**Agents need supervision.** An agent can make mistakes. A web page can contain hidden instructions that try to trick it. Watch what it does. Do not hand over passwords. Approve purchases yourself.

**Usage limits can interrupt you.** Free and lower plans have caps. Long sessions can hit them. If that happens often, that is the sign to consider Plus, or to split the work across days.

**Features change fast.** Names and buttons move. Some features launch in the US first. If a button in this chapter is missing, check the tools menu. It may have moved.

**Do not share secrets.** Do not paste passwords, bank details, or medical records into a normal chat. Use a temporary chat for sensitive questions, or keep them out entirely.

## Check yourself

1. You want ChatGPT to always know your renovation budget and floor plans. What feature fits?

**Answer:** A Project. Add instructions and upload the files. Every chat inside the project starts with that context.

2. How do you ask a sensitive question without it being saved to history or memory?

**Answer:** Use a temporary chat. Toggle it at the top of a new chat.

3. What is replacing custom GPTs?

**Answer:** Plugins, which combine reusable instructions called skills with connected apps. Custom GPTs stop running on December 11, 2026.

4. Agent mode found the perfect hotel and wants to book it. What should you do?

**Answer:** Review the details yourself first. Approve the booking only when you are sure. Agents can make mistakes.

5. An answer to a hard question seems shallow. What should you try?

**Answer:** Switch to Thinking mode and ask again, or say "think harder about this."

## In short

- ChatGPT is the all-rounder: chat, voice, vision, images, search, deep research, and memory.
- Use Auto most of the time. Use Thinking for hard problems.
- Projects keep a topic organized with shared instructions and files.
- Agent mode, ChatGPT Work, and scheduled tasks let it do multi-step jobs. Supervise them.
- Custom GPTs are retiring. Plugins and skills replace them.
- Check Data controls, Memory, and temporary chats for privacy.

# 2.10 Mistral, start to finish
<!-- original: ch. 26 -->

Mistral's assistant is called **Vibe**. It used to be called Le Chat. Mistral renamed it on May 28, 2026. Your chats and subscription carried over. The phone apps are still listed as "Vibe by Mistral AI" in the app stores.

Vibe is the assistant from Mistral AI, France's AI champion and Europe's leading AI lab. It is fast. Very fast. It is capable, generous on its free tier, and fairly priced. It comes with memories, connectors, deep research, image creation, and document reading.

For anyone who wants a strong alternative to the American giants, or prefers a company under European privacy rules, Vibe is well worth a look.

## Getting started

You need a free account at chat.mistral.ai.

1. Go to **chat.mistral.ai** or install **Vibe by Mistral AI**.
2. Sign up with email, Google, Apple, or Microsoft.
3. Turn on **Memories** if you want personalized answers. You can even **import your ChatGPT memories** in one click.
4. Browse **Connectors** to link the apps you use.

A **memory** is a fact Vibe saves about you, like your job or your food preferences. It uses memories to personalize answers. You can view, edit, and delete every memory.

> NOTE: Vibe is the new name. Older guides call it Le Chat. It is the same assistant. If a guide says Le Chat, it means Vibe.

## What it is best at

**Speed.** Vibe answers very quickly. That makes rapid back-and-forth editing a joy. Draft, revise, revise again. No waiting around.

**Document understanding.** Vibe is excellent at reading PDFs and scans, including tables and handwriting. Mistral built strong **OCR** technology. OCR means reading text from images and scans. Try uploading a photo of a handwritten recipe or a printed form. Ask: "Transcribe this exactly, then turn it into a clean, formatted version."

**Think mode.** This is Vibe's reasoning mode for harder problems. It works in many languages. Use it for logic, maths, and careful comparisons.

**Web search.** Answers come with sources, including news from partner outlets. Ask for recent facts and check the links.

**Deep Research.** Multi-step research reports with citations. Ask a big question and let Vibe work through it.

**Canvas.** Co-write and edit documents or code side by side with Vibe.

**Image creation.** Generate and edit images from descriptions. Good for illustrations, posters, and fun.

**Projects.** Organize chats, files, and instructions by topic. Like Projects in ChatGPT and Claude, but in Vibe.

**Multilingual help.** Vibe is great for French, German, Spanish, Italian, and more. Try: "Write a polite message to my neighbor in French explaining we will have builders on Monday and Tuesday. Then give me the English translation."

The models behind Vibe are Mistral's own. They include Large 3, Medium 3.5, and a reasoning model called Magistral. Mistral also publishes open-weight models. Large 4 was released as open-weight on October 29, 2026.

> TRY IT: Upload a photo of a handwritten note or an old recipe card. Ask Vibe to transcribe and tidy it. Then ask the same in another assistant. Which one read the handwriting better?

## Memories and connectors

**Memories** save useful facts and preferences. You will see when Vibe saves one. You can view, edit, and delete every memory. You can turn memories off entirely if you prefer.

Switching from ChatGPT? Go to Memories, then **Import from ChatGPT**, and follow the steps. Your saved facts move across in about a minute. Then ask, "What do you know about me now?" Tidy anything outdated.

**Connectors** link Vibe to your other apps. They are built on **MCP**, the open standard for connecting AI to apps. Search, summarize, and act across tools like Asana and Outlook. Or add your own custom MCP connector. Vibe asks you to approve actions before they happen.

**Recipes to try.** First, read a messy scanned document: upload a photo or scan of a handwritten recipe or a printed form, and ask Vibe to transcribe it exactly, then turn it into a clean, formatted version. Second, switch from ChatGPT in a minute: go to Memories, pick Import from ChatGPT, follow the steps, then ask "What do you know about me now?" and tidy anything outdated. Third, use Vibe as a multilingual helper: ask it to write a polite message to a neighbor in French explaining builders will be there Monday and Tuesday, then give you the English translation. Check the French with a speaker if the message matters.

**Speed as a habit.** Because Vibe answers so fast, it suits quick drafting loops. Write a rough paragraph, ask Vibe to tighten it, then ask for a shorter version, then a warmer version. Three rounds take a minute. That is the workflow Vibe is built for: fast, small, repeated edits, not one giant perfect answer.

**Open models for builders.** Mistral publishes many of its models openly, so developers can download and run them. Large 4 was released as open-weight on October 29, 2026. That openness is part of Mistral's identity. It means the same technology that powers Vibe can also run on a company's own computers, or inside other apps. Everyday users do not need to care about this. But it is why Vibe appears in so many other tools, and why it is a favorite second opinion: the same brains, in many places.

## Privacy and settings

Mistral is a European company. It follows the **GDPR** and the **EU AI Act**. The GDPR is Europe's strict privacy law. It gives you rights over your data, including the right to see it and delete it.

- **Training opt-out.** Turn off use of your conversations for training in settings. Business plans do not train on your data by default.
- **Memories.** Fully viewable and deletable. Turn them off entirely if you prefer.
- **Enterprise self-hosting.** Organizations can run Vibe on their own infrastructure. That means their own computers, under their own control.

For builders, Mistral's open-weight models and its coding agent, also called Mistral Vibe in some places, are popular with developers. Do not confuse the coding agent with the chat assistant. Same family, different tool.

## Traps to avoid

**A smaller ecosystem.** Vibe has fewer integrations than Google or Microsoft. If your life runs on Gmail or Office, Gemini or Copilot may fit better. Vibe works best as a main assistant for people who want speed and privacy, or as a second opinion.

**Voice, video, and agent features are less mature.** The biggest rivals are further ahead here. Vibe is catching up, but if voice is your main way of using AI, try ChatGPT or Gemini first.

**Names can confuse.** The assistant is Vibe. The company is Mistral AI. The models have names like Large and Magistral. Older guides say Le Chat. It is all the same family.

**Check the current price.** Prices change by country. The Student and Pro prices above are US list prices from November 2026. Confirm them on Mistral's site before you pay.

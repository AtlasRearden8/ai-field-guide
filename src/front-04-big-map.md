# The big map of AI, on one page

AI feels huge. It helps to see the whole field on one page. Here it is. Keep this page as a bookmark. When you feel lost, come back and find where you are.

## The four layers

Everything in AI sits in one of four layers. They stack like a cake.

| Layer | What it is | Examples | Learn it in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Models | The "brains" that read and write | GPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Llama, DeepSeek | Section 1, Chapter 1.2 |
| Apps | Where you talk to the brains | ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Notion, Word | Section 2 |
| Your stuff | The files, notes, and memory AI can use | Notes, email, calendar, photos, documents | Section 3 |
| Doing things | AI that acts, not just answers | Automations, agents, coding tools, connectors | Section 4 |

You start at the top. You talk to a model through an app. Later, you let it see your stuff. Later still, you let it do things for you. Each layer builds on the one above it.

> NOTE: You do not need the bottom layers to get value from AI. Most people live happily in the top two layers for months. Move down only when you want more.

## What makes AI useful to you

AI is useful in four ways. Every use in this book is one of these.

- **It writes.** Drafts, emails, messages, stories, lists, plans.
- **It explains.** Hard documents, new topics, confusing letters, in plain words.
- **It finds.** Answers from your notes, summaries of long text, research from the web.
- **It does.** Reminders, bookings, files sorted, chores run on a schedule.

If you are ever stuck, ask: "Which of the four do I need right now?" Then ask the AI for exactly that.

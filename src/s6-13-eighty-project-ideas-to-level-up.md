# 6.13 Eighty project ideas to level up
<!-- original: app. F -->

Reading is great, but building is where it sticks. Here are 80 projects, from a weekend afternoon to ambitious. Every one teaches something real, and most link to a chapter or build-along that shows you how.

**How to pick.** Choose something that fixes an actual annoyance in your life, or makes someone you love smile. You will finish it. A project you care about beats a project that merely sounds impressive.

Starter projects take an afternoon and need no code. Intermediate projects take a weekend with light setup. Advanced projects take a few weekends, with code written with AI help. Ambitious projects are stretch goals. The last group is sorted by passion, so you can find the ones that fit you.

## Starter: an afternoon, no code, 1–15

1. **Chat with your Drive.** Ask questions across your documents with Claude and the Google Drive connector. You will learn connectors and grounded answers.
2. **Study podcast.** Turn your notes into an audio overview with Gemini Notebook. You will learn grounding and multimodal output.
3. **AI folder cleanup.** Sort a messy folder with Claude Desktop and the Filesystem MCP server. You will learn local MCP and permissions.
4. **Model tasting night.** Try three assistants with the same ten prompts and compare the answers. You will learn how to judge AI tools.
5. **Birthday song.** Make a personalised birthday song with Claude and Suno. You will learn lyrics, structure tags, and song generation.
6. **Pet portrait gallery.** Make five portraits of your pet in different art styles. You will learn image prompts and style words.
7. **Fridge meals.** Photograph your fridge and ask for five meals from what you see. You will learn multimodal inputs.
8. **Voice interview.** Let voice mode interview you for a job, then give you feedback. You will learn voice AI.
9. **Bedtime story book.** Co-write a story with your child and illustrate it. You will learn storytelling with AI.
10. **Travel plan.** Plan a weekend trip with an assistant, including hidden spots. You will learn research with AI.
11. **Inbox zero day.** Let AI draft replies to your oldest unread emails. You will learn email triage.
12. **Recipe remix.** Give Claude your fridge contents and invent three new dishes. You will learn creative prompting.
13. **Photo restorer.** Restore and colourise an old family photo. You will learn image editing by chatting.
14. **Language buddy.** Practise a language for fifteen minutes a day with voice mode. You will learn conversational AI.
15. **Personal FAQ.** Write a page of answers to questions people always ask you. You will learn to organise knowledge.

## A 30-day go deeper plan

Pick one starter project and finish it in week one. Pick one intermediate project and finish it in week two. Pick one advanced project and start it in week three. Share what you built in week four.

Share what you build. Post in communities such as r/ClaudeAI, r/n8n, r/LocalLLaMA, or the MCP Discord. People love seeing real projects, and you will learn tons from the replies.

> NOTE: The best project is the one you finish. A small project done beats a big project started. Pick one, set a deadline, and ship it. Then pick the next one.

> TRY IT: Read the list once and circle three ideas that excite you. Pick the smallest one. Start it today, even if you only have thirty minutes. A started project is a project that can be finished.

## Check yourself

- **Q1:** How should you pick a project from this list?
**Answer:** Choose one that fixes a real annoyance in your life, or makes someone you love smile, because you will finish it.

- **Q2:** What is the difference between a starter and an intermediate project?
**Answer:** A starter takes an afternoon and needs no code, while an intermediate takes a weekend with light setup.

- **Q3:** What should you do after you finish a project?
**Answer:** Share it in a community, so others can learn from it and you can learn from their replies.

## In short

- Eighty projects, from an afternoon to ambitious, sorted by difficulty and by passion.
- Starter projects need no code; intermediate ones need a weekend; advanced ones need code with AI help.

- Finish one small project before you start a big one.

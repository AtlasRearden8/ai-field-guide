# 2.5 Copilot, start to finish
<!-- original: ch. 21 -->

Copilot is Microsoft's AI assistant. It lives where millions of people already work: Windows, the Edge browser, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams.

In 2026 Microsoft merged its consumer Copilot app and its Microsoft 365 Copilot app into one **Microsoft Copilot** app. The same assistant now helps with everyday questions and with your documents and email.

If you use a Windows PC or Office for work or school, Copilot is often the most convenient assistant you have.

## Getting started

You need a Microsoft account. That is the same login you use for Outlook, Xbox, or Office.

1. Open Copilot. Press the **Copilot key** on your Windows keyboard, or tap the Copilot icon on the taskbar. On your phone or Mac, open the Microsoft Copilot app. On the web, go to the Copilot website.
2. Sign in with your **Microsoft account** for personal use, or your **work or school account** for work. Signing in saves your history and unlocks more features.
3. Personalize it. Go to Settings, then **Personalization** or **Memory**. Let Copilot remember your preferences, or turn memory off.
4. Turn on voice if you want it. On Windows you can enable "Hey Copilot" for hands-free use.

A **Microsoft account** is one login for all Microsoft services. If you have Outlook email or an Xbox, you already have one.

> NOTE: Personal accounts and work accounts see different features. That confuses a lot of people. At work, always sign in with your work account. At home, use your personal account. Do not mix them.

**What changed in August 2026**

Microsoft combined its consumer and business Copilot apps into one app. The merge rolled out from mid-August on mobile and web, and mid-September on Windows and Mac.

Along the way, Microsoft retired several consumer features: **Group Chats, Copilot Podcasts, Copilot Labs, and consumer Deep Research**. The animated **Mico** character left standard voice mode. It lives on in the **Learn Live** tutoring experience. Deep research lives on as **Researcher** for Microsoft 365 Premium subscribers.

A refreshed Copilot arrived on September 25, 2026. It added Home, Code, and Autopilot. If an old guide mentions a missing feature, this is why.

## What it is best at

**Everyday chat, voice, and vision.** Ask anything. Upload files and photos. Tap the microphone for a spoken conversation.

**Copilot Vision** is the standout. Share your **screen** on Windows, or your **camera** on mobile. Ask: "How do I turn off these notifications?" Or: "What is the best move in this game?" Or: "Which of these laptops is better value?" Vision highlights where to click. It is brilliant for tech help.

**Image creation.** Say "Create an image of..." right in the chat. It makes pictures for invitations, slides, and fun.

**Learn Live.** This is a tutoring mode. It teaches step by step, with questions and visuals. Good for students and for adults learning something new.

**Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook.** With a Microsoft 365 plan, look for the Copilot button in each app.

- **Word.** Draft a letter, rewrite a paragraph, or summarize a long document.
- **Excel.** Ask for formulas in plain words. Categorize transactions. Make a chart of where your money goes.
- **PowerPoint.** Create a presentation from a document. Ask for eight slides, simpler words, and speaker notes.
- **Outlook.** Summarize a long thread. Draft a reply in your tone.

In Microsoft 365 apps, type **/** to pick a file to reference. Try: "Summarize /Q3 report and compare it to /Q2 report."

**Copilot in Edge and Windows.** Open Copilot in the Edge sidebar. Summarize pages, compare products across tabs, or draft text. Edge's **Copilot Mode** turns the browser into an AI-first experience. It can help with multi-step browsing tasks, with your permission.

In Windows, ask Copilot to explain settings, find files, or help with apps. Share your screen with Vision. On **Copilot+ PCs**, features like smarter Windows search and **Click to Do** run partly on your device. Click to Do offers actions on whatever is on your screen.

**Researcher and Analyst.** These are on Premium and business plans.

- **Researcher** does multi-step research across the web, and your work files on business plans. It produces a detailed, cited report.
- **Analyst** is a data-focused agent. It works through spreadsheets and data files, runs analysis, and explains what it found.

> TRY IT: Press the Copilot key, or open the app. Share your screen with Vision. Ask it to help you tidy one thing on your computer: notifications, startup apps, or storage. Tech chores, done in five minutes.

**Copilot in Teams.** If your work uses Teams, Copilot can summarize a meeting you missed. It can list the decisions and the action items. It can also draft a follow-up message. Ask it after the meeting: "Summarize this meeting in five bullet points. Who agreed to do what?" Check the names and dates before you share the summary. Copilot can mishear a name, and a wrong name in a work summary causes real confusion.

## Step-by-step recipes

**Recipe 1: Fix a Windows annoyance with Vision**

1. Open Copilot and share your screen. Pick the Settings window.
2. Ask: "My laptop keeps waking up at night. What should I change here?"
3. Follow the highlighted steps, one at a time. Ask Copilot to explain any step you do not understand.

**Recipe 2: Budget spreadsheet in Excel**

1. Open your spending sheet and click Copilot.
2. Ask: "Categorize these transactions, total each category, and make a chart of where my money goes."
3. Check the categories. If one is wrong, say so. Copilot will fix it.

**Recipe 3: Inbox rescue in Outlook**

1. Open a long email thread and click **Summarize**.
2. Then ask: "Draft a reply confirming I will send the report by Friday and asking for the latest figures."
3. Read the draft before you send it. Fix the tone if it sounds too stiff.

**Recipe 4: From document to deck**

1. In PowerPoint, click Copilot and say: "Create a presentation from this document."
2. Then say: "Make it 8 slides, simpler words, and add speaker notes."
3. Check every slide. Copilot can shorten a slide too much, so look for missing points.

## Traps to avoid

**Features come and go.** Microsoft adds and retires features often. The merged app is still evolving. Do not be surprised if a button moves or a feature disappears. Check the current help pages when something is missing.

**Personal versus work accounts.** The two accounts see different features. Work data stays in the work account. If Copilot cannot see a file, check which account you signed in with.

**Office features need a plan.** Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook needs a Microsoft 365 subscription. The free chat app alone will not unlock them.

**Privacy settings to check.** Go to Settings and look at Personalization and Memory. Decide what Copilot may remember. At work, your company sets the rules. For personal use, turn off memory if you prefer a fresh start each time.

**Do not paste secrets.** Do not share passwords or bank details with Copilot. Keep sensitive work documents in your work account, not your personal one.

## Check yourself

1. What is the easiest way to get step-by-step help with a confusing Windows setting?

**Answer:** Open Copilot and use Copilot Vision to share your screen, then ask what to change.

2. You want Copilot inside Excel at home. What do you need?

**Answer:** A Microsoft 365 plan, such as Personal, Family, or Premium.

3. Your old guide mentions Copilot Podcasts, but you cannot find them. Why?

**Answer:** Microsoft retired Podcasts, along with Group Chats, Labs, and consumer Deep Research, in August 2026.

4. Which Copilot tool turns a document into a presentation?

**Answer:** Copilot in PowerPoint. Ask it to create a presentation from the document.

5. What are Researcher and Analyst?

**Answer:** Researcher does multi-step research and writes cited reports. Analyst works through spreadsheets and data files and explains the findings. Both come with Microsoft 365 Premium and business plans.

## In short

- Copilot is the natural choice for Windows and Microsoft 365 users.
- Copilot Vision, screen and camera sharing, makes it a superb tech-help buddy.
- Microsoft 365 plans unlock Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and OneNote.
- Premium adds Researcher and Analyst.
- In August 2026, Group Chats, Podcasts, Labs, and consumer Deep Research were retired and the apps merged.
- Use your work account for work data. Check privacy settings for personal use.

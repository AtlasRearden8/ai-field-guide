# Appendix B — The cheat sheet

One page of reminders for the whole book. Keep it open while you work.

## The golden rules

- Give background first, then ask the question.
- Say what you want back: length, tone, and format.
- Check facts that matter. AI can sound sure and still be wrong.
- Never share passwords, bank details, or private health facts.
- Start small. One tiny win beats one big plan.
- Save good prompts. Reuse them.

## A good prompt, in five parts

| Part | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Role | Who should the AI act as? |
| Job | What do you want it to do? |
| Background | What does it need to know? |
| Format | List, table, email, or short paragraph? |
| Limits | How long? What tone? What to leave out? |

## Talk to AI like a teammate

- Ask one thing at a time.
- Follow up: "Shorter, please" or "Explain that like I am ten."
- Correct it: "That is wrong. The date is Tuesday."
- Ask for options: "Give me three ideas."
- Ask for a check: "What are you unsure about here?"

## Pick the right assistant

| Job | Good first pick |
| --- | --- |
| Everyday chat and writing | ChatGPT or Claude |
| Google files and search | Gemini |
| Work in Microsoft tools | Copilot |
| Research with sources | Perplexity |
| Fast, cheap, open | DeepSeek or Mistral |
| Built into your phone | Apple Intelligence, Siri, or Alexa+ |

## Before you trust an answer

- Is it a fact, or a guess?
- Does it name a source you can open?
- Would a wrong answer cost you money, health, or safety?
- If yes to the last one, check it somewhere else first.

## Safety in ten seconds

- No passwords, no card numbers, no ID numbers.
- No photos of children with names and schools.
- No work secrets in a personal account.
- If a message begs you to act fast, stop and check.
- A voice on the phone can be faked. Call back on a known number.

## Save money

- Use the free plan until you hit its limits.
- Use a cheaper model for short, simple jobs.
- Use a strong model only for hard jobs.
- Turn off tools you do not use.
- Check the current price before you pay for a plan.

## When AI gets it wrong

- Ask it to try again, in a different way.
- Give it the source text to work from.
- Break a big job into small steps.
- Ask: "Show your working."
- Switch assistants and compare the answers.

## Files AI likes

| File type | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| Plain text and Word | Reading, editing, and summaries |
| PDF | Reports, manuals, and forms |
| Spreadsheet | Tables, numbers, and lists |
| Image | Describing, reading text, and ideas |
| Audio | Transcripts and summaries |

## Your first week, at a glance

| Day | Tiny win |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Have your first chat. |
| 2 | Write one email with AI. |
| 3 | Plan one meal from what is in the fridge. |
| 4 | Summarise one long article. |
| 5 | Make one list or plan. |
| 6 | Ask AI to explain one thing you never understood. |
| 7 | Teach someone else one trick you learned. |

## If you only remember three things

- Be clear about what you want.
- Check what matters.
- Keep private things private.

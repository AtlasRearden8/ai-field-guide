# Appendix C — Troubleshooting FAQ

It broke. Now what? Find your problem below. Try the fixes in order.

## The AI will not answer me at all

Check your internet first. Then refresh the page or close and reopen the app. If it still will not answer, sign out and sign back in. If the service itself is down, wait a short while and try again.

## I get an error that says "something went wrong"

This is a catch-all error. Refresh the page. Start a new chat. Try a shorter message. If it keeps happening, the service may be busy. Try again in a few minutes.

## The page will not load

Check other sites. If they load, the problem is with the AI site, not you. Clear your browser cache or try a different browser. On a phone, close the app fully and reopen it.

## I cannot sign in

Check your email address for a typo. Use the "forgot password" link if you need a new one. If you signed up with Google or Apple, use that same button. Do not make a second account by mistake.

## I forgot my password

Use the reset link on the sign-in page. Check your spam folder for the reset email. The link often expires after a short time, so use it straight away.

## The app says I have hit my limit

Free plans cap how many messages you can send. Wait until the limit resets, usually in a few hours. Or switch to a different free assistant for now. Check the current price before you pay to lift the limit.

## The answer is wrong

Do not trust it. Give the AI the correct facts and ask it to try again. Ask for sources. Break the job into smaller steps. Check the key facts somewhere you trust.

## The AI made up a fact, a link, or a quote

This is called a hallucination. It happens when the model guesses. Ask it to use only the text you gave it. Ask for a source you can open. If the link does not open, the source is not real.

## The answer is too long

Say: "Shorter, please. Three bullet points." Or set a limit in your prompt, such as "in under 100 words". Save the short version and move on.

## The answer is too short or too vague

Give more background. Say what you want back and how much detail you need. Ask a follow-up: "Give me an example for each point."

## The AI will not follow my format

Show it the format you want. Paste a short example. Say: "Use exactly this layout." If it still drifts, ask for one section at a time.

## The AI keeps repeating itself

Start a new chat. The old chat may be steering it. Give a fresh, short prompt with clear limits. Ask for new ideas only.

## The AI sounds rude, or the tone is wrong

Tell it the tone you want: "Friendly and plain, like talking to a neighbour." Give one sentence in the tone you like. It will copy that style.

## It will not write in my language

Say the language by name: "Answer in Welsh." If it slips back to English, repeat the instruction at the start of the next message. Keep your own message in that language too.

## The AI refuses to help

Some requests are blocked for safety. If your request is harmless, reword it. Be specific about what you want. Do not try to trick it into breaking its rules.

## My file will not upload

Check the file size and type. Many tools cap uploads at a set size. Try a PDF or plain text version. Split a very large file into smaller parts. Rename the file without odd symbols.

## The AI cannot read my PDF

The PDF may be a photo of text, not real text. Try a different file. Or copy and paste the text in. Or take a clear photo of the page and upload that instead.

## The AI cannot see my image clearly

Upload a larger, sharper image. Crop to the part that matters. Describe what is in the image yourself, in one sentence, to help it.

## Voice input does not work

Check the microphone permission in your browser or phone settings. Speak clearly, in a quiet room. Try typing the same question to check the service works at all.

## The AI misunderstands my voice

Speak more slowly. Use short sentences. Say names and numbers one at a time. If it keeps failing, type instead.

## My chat history has vanished

Check you are signed in to the right account. Look in archived chats or the history menu. If you used a guest chat, it may not be saved. From now on, sign in before you start an important chat.

## I deleted a chat by mistake

Most tools have no undo for a deleted chat. Check the trash or archive folder if there is one. If it is gone, start again and save the key answers in your notes this time.

## The AI is very slow today

Long answers take longer. So do busy times of day. Try a shorter prompt. Try a faster, cheaper model. Close other tabs and apps that may be slowing your device.

## It costs more than I expected

Check your plan and your usage page. API use is billed per message, and long files cost more. Set a spending cap if the tool offers one. Use the free plan or a cheaper model for simple jobs.

## I was charged but I did not mean to pay

Check your email for a receipt. Cancel the plan in the account settings so it does not renew. Ask the provider for a refund through their help page. Keep the receipt.

## My code does not run

Copy the error message exactly and paste it back to the AI. Say what you expected to happen. Ask: "Fix this error, and explain the fix in plain words." Test the fix in small steps.

## The AI wrote code that looks right but does nothing

Ask it to explain what each part does. Run it one piece at a time. Check that the file names and tools match yours. A tiny typo can break the whole thing.

## My automation runs twice, or never runs

Check the trigger. A trigger is the event that starts the job. Check the schedule and the time zone. Look at the run log to see where it stopped. Turn the workflow off, fix one thing, then turn it on again.

## My automation worked once, then stopped

Check for a changed password, an expired login, or a changed file name. Connections break when any of these change. Reconnect the tool and test the workflow by hand.

## The AI connected to the wrong account or file

Disconnect the tool. Reconnect it and pick the right account slowly. Check the file name and folder before you run the job. Name files clearly so this is easy to spot.

## I am worried the AI shared my private data

Stop using that chat for private things. Check the privacy settings. Turn off training on your data if the tool offers that. Do not paste passwords, health details, or bank numbers into any chatbot.

## Something looks like a scam

Do not click the link. Do not reply. If it claims to be your bank, call the bank on a number you already know. Report the message in the app where you got it.

## A photo or voice of someone I know seems fake

It may be a deepfake. Call the person on a known number. Ask them something only they would know. Do not send money or share codes because a voice told you to.

## I cannot tell which assistant or plan to pick

Start with a free plan. Use it for a week on real jobs. Keep notes on what worked. Pick the one that felt easiest. You can switch later. There is no wrong first pick.

## The tool I want is not in this book

Tools change fast. Search for the tool by name and check its official site. Check the current price and the free plan. The skills in this book still apply to new tools.

## I feel stuck and do not know what to ask

Copy a prompt from Appendix D and change the details to fit your job. Or start with: "I want to do X. I am a beginner. Walk me through it, one step at a time."

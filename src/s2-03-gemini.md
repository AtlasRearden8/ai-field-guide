# 2.3 Gemini, start to finish
<!-- original: ch. 19 -->

Gemini is Google's AI assistant. Its superpower is Google itself.

It lives in Android phones, Chrome, Gmail, Docs, Google Home, and more. If you let it, it can use your own Gmail, Calendar, Photos, and Drive to give answers that are truly personal.

Add live voice and camera, image and video creation, Deep Research, and custom Gems. You have one of the most capable assistants around.

## Getting started

You need a Google account. That is all.

1. On Android, long-press the power button or say "Hey Google." On iPhone, install the Google Gemini app. On a computer, go to **gemini.google.com**.
2. Sign in with your Google account.
3. Tell it about you. Go to Settings, then **Personal context** or **Saved info**. Add facts like "I am vegetarian" or "Keep answers short."
4. Choose connected apps. Go to Settings, then **Apps**, or use the Tools menu. Pick which Google apps Gemini can use.
5. Leave the model picker on the default to start. It offers faster and more thoughtful modes. The default is fine for most questions.

A **connected app** is a Google app you allow Gemini to read. Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Photos, Maps, and YouTube are the main ones. You can turn each one on or off.

> NOTE: The free Gemini app now runs a lighter model, called Flash-Lite, since October 9, 2026. Paid plans unlock the stronger models. If answers feel thin on the free plan, that is why. The paid plan is Google AI Pro.

**Plans in plain English**

Gemini plans are sold through Google One. They often include cloud storage for the whole family. Students often get discounts. Some new Pixel phones come with trial months.

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Chat with the lighter Flash-Lite model, with limits |
| Google AI Plus | $4.99 a month | A stronger model and twice the free usage |
| Google AI Pro | $19.99 a month, or $199.99 a year | The strongest models, Deep Research, Veo video, 5 TB of storage |
| Google AI Ultra | $99.99 or $199.99 a month | The highest limits, for heavy users |

US prices, checked in November 2026. Check the current price on Google's site. Prices change by country.

## What it is best at

**Personal Intelligence.** This is Gemini's headline feature. It is opt-in. That means you choose to turn it on.

Personal Intelligence lets Gemini draw on your Google apps and your past chats. Then you can ask things only you could answer before:

- "When does my flight to Rome land, and how do I get from the airport to my hotel?"
- "What was the name of that pasta recipe video I watched last week?"
- "Find the photo of our car's license plate."
- "What have I promised people this week?"
- "Draft a reply to Jamie's email about the school fundraiser."

You choose which apps are connected. You can switch it off for a single prompt from the Tools menu. It is available in many countries, though not yet in Europe.

Gemini can also make a **Daily Brief**. That is a short morning overview of your priorities, from Gmail, Calendar, and your chats. Ask, "Give me my daily brief."

**Gemini Live.** Tap **Live**, the waveform icon, for a flowing voice conversation. Then point your camera at something. Ask about the back of your TV, your garden, or your fridge. Share your screen and ask for help comparing two phone plans. Live can also work with your apps. It can add calendar events, create reminders, draft emails, and get directions.

**Gems.** A **Gem** is a custom version of Gemini with saved instructions and optional files. Make one for meal planning, homework help, or a writing editor.

1. In the sidebar, pick **Gems**, then **New Gem**.
2. Name it and write instructions. Say who you are, what it should do, and what format you want.
3. Add knowledge files if you like. A family's favourite recipes, for example.
4. Save it. Chat with it any time from the sidebar.

Google also includes premade Gems. A learning coach, a brainstormer, a career guide, and a writing editor are ready to try or copy.

**Deep Research and Canvas.** Pick Deep Research from the Tools menu. Ask a big question. Approve the research plan. In a few minutes you get a detailed, cited report. It can include your own Gmail, Drive, and Chat content if you allow it. You can turn a report into an **Audio Overview**. That is a podcast-style summary with two AI hosts. Or make a quiz or an infographic from it.

Canvas is for co-writing. Pick Canvas from Tools. Co-write documents, generate web pages, quizzes, infographics, and simple apps. Edit them with Gemini side by side. Export documents to Google Docs.

**Images and video.** Google's image models, nicknamed **Nano Banana**, are excellent at editing your own photos. They keep people and pets looking like themselves. Try: "Put me and my sister on a beach at sunset." Or: "Restore and colorize this old family photo."

**Veo** creates short, realistic video clips with sound. Describe a scene, or start from a photo. Try: "A golden retriever surfing a small wave, slow motion, sunny day." Paid plans get more video.

All Google AI images and videos carry an invisible **SynthID** watermark. That is a hidden mark that says the image was made by AI. Gemini can check whether an image was made with Google AI.

> TRY IT: Start Gemini Live, turn on your camera, and give it a tour of one room. Ask: "What would you change to make this room feel bigger?" Then create a Gem for something you do every week, like meal planning or a homework helper.

## Gemini inside your Google apps

This is where Gemini earns its keep. It works where you already work.

- **Gmail.** Summarize long threads. Draft replies in your tone. Find a booking or an address.
- **Docs and Sheets.** Draft, rewrite, summarize, and ask questions about a document. In Sheets, ask for formulas in plain words.
- **Chrome.** Ask Gemini about the page you are reading. Summarize it. Compare products across tabs.
- **Google Home and Nest.** **Gemini for Home** is replacing Google Assistant on Nest speakers and displays. It holds more natural conversations. It controls smart home devices better. Try: "Turn off everything downstairs except the porch light."
- **Android Auto.** Hands-free messages, directions, and questions while you drive.
- **Maps and YouTube.** Ask for directions and place details. Paste a video link and ask for a summary or the key steps.

**Agents and scheduled actions.** Say: "Every morning at 7, summarize my calendar and any urgent emails." Or: "Every Friday, send me three weekend ideas near me." Newer agent features, including **Spark**, work on tasks across your digital life. They roll out first to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in the US.

**Gemini Notebook.** This used to be called NotebookLM. Upload sources like PDFs, websites, YouTube videos, and notes. Get answers grounded in your sources. Make study guides, mind maps, flashcards, and Audio Overviews. **Guided Learning** is a tutor mode. It teaches step by step and checks your understanding.

**For builders.** Google AI Studio lets you try models for free and build apps by describing them. The Gemini CLI and Google's coding agents help with coding. The Gemini API is for developers building their own apps.

## Traps to avoid

**Personal answers are only as good as your connected data.** If Gemini misreads an email, the answer will be wrong. Check anything important.

**Features vary a lot by country, age, and plan.** Personal Intelligence is not in Europe yet. Some features launch in the US first. If a feature is missing, check your plan and your country.

**Human review.** Google says a sample of conversations may be reviewed by trained reviewers to improve its products. The sample is disconnected from your account. Even so, do not enter confidential facts you would not want a reviewer to see. Or turn off activity saving.

**It can be confidently wrong.** Like every assistant, Gemini can state a false fact with total confidence. Use the double-check option. It highlights claims that Google Search supports or contradicts.

**Privacy settings to check.** Go to **Gemini Apps Activity**. This controls whether Google keeps your chats and uses them to improve its AI. You can set auto-delete there. Work and school Google Workspace accounts have stronger protections than personal accounts.

## Check yourself

1. You want Gemini to answer "When does my flight land?" from your booking email. What needs to be on?

**Answer:** Personal Intelligence, with Gmail connected, in a country where it is available.

2. What is the Gemini version of a custom assistant with saved instructions?

**Answer:** A Gem. Make one in the sidebar, under Gems, then New Gem.

3. Which setting controls whether Google keeps your Gemini chats and uses them to improve its AI?

**Answer:** Gemini Apps Activity, also called Keep Activity. You can set auto-delete there.

4. You want a short video with sound from a description. Which Gemini tool makes it?

**Answer:** Veo. It creates short, realistic video clips with sound.

5. What is the Daily Brief?

**Answer:** A short morning overview of your priorities, built from your Gmail, Calendar, and chats.

## In short

- Gemini's superpower is Google integration: Gmail, Calendar, Photos, Maps, YouTube, Chrome, Android, and Home.
- Personal Intelligence, opt-in, answers questions about your own life.
- Gemini Live adds voice, camera, and screen sharing. Gems are your custom assistants.
- Deep Research, Canvas, Nano Banana images, and Veo video are standout tools.
- Manage privacy with Gemini Apps Activity, connected apps, and temporary chats.
- The free plan runs the lighter Flash-Lite model. Google AI Pro costs $19.99 a month.

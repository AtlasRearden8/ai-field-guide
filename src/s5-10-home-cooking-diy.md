# 5.10 Home, cooking, and DIY with AI
<!-- original: ch. 100 -->

Your home is full of small jobs. What is for dinner? How do I fix this tap? When does the boiler need a service? AI can help with all of them. It is a pocket chef, a fix-it guide, and a project planner in one.

The rule for this chapter is simple. AI advises. You decide. A recipe can be checked by tasting. A repair can be checked by looking. A plan can be checked by measuring. Use AI to start. Use your own eyes and hands to finish.

## Cooking: your pocket chef

Cooking is the most loved AI use in the home. Start with the fridge. List what you have. Tell AI how many people are eating, how long you have, and what you cannot eat. Ask for three dinner ideas. Pick one. Ask for the recipe with clear steps and times.

AI can adapt recipes. Make it vegetarian. Make it for two instead of six. Use chicken instead of beef. Swap an ingredient you lack. Each change is a question away. Check the adapted recipe before you cook. AI can get quantities wrong. A missing zero turns a teaspoon into a tablespoon. Taste as you go.

Meal planning saves the week. Ask AI to plan five dinners from one shopping trip. Ask for a shopping list sorted by shop section. Ask for a plan that uses leftovers. Sunday's roast becomes Monday's sandwiches. Planning once beats deciding every night at six o'clock.

Baking needs more care. Baking is chemistry. Quantities matter. Ask AI for a tested recipe and follow it closely. Do not improvise with baking powder. Improvise with seasoning instead.

> NOTE: AI recipes can be wrong about cooking times and temperatures, and they can miss allergens. Check every recipe for your allergies, and use a food thermometer for meat where it matters.

## DIY and repairs

DIY means doing home repairs and improvements yourself. AI is a good teacher for this. Describe the problem. "The tap drips when I turn it off." Ask what the likely cause is. Ask what tools you need. Ask for steps in order. Ask what could go wrong.

Safety comes first. Some jobs are not for beginners. Gas. Mains electricity. Structural walls. Roofs. If AI says "call a professional" for your job, listen. A saved call-out fee is not worth a flood or a fire. If you are unsure, ask a human expert before you start.

For safe jobs, AI helps you prepare. Ask for a tool list. Ask for a materials list with rough quantities. Ask for the steps. Ask how long each step takes. Then watch a video as well. Reading plus watching beats reading alone. Stop if something does not match. If the pipe looks different from the description, stop and ask a professional.

Ask AI to estimate costs. But treat the estimate as a guess. Check the current price of materials at a real shop before you buy. Prices change. AI may quote old ones.

> TRY IT: Pick one small repair you have been avoiding, like a squeaky door or a loose handle. Ask AI for the cause, the tools, and the steps. Gather the tools first. Then do the job. Most small fixes take under twenty minutes once you know the steps.

## Home maintenance, gardening, and decorating

Home maintenance is the boring schedule that saves money. Boilers. Gutters. Smoke alarms. Filters. Ask AI to build a yearly calendar. What needs doing each month? What can you do yourself? What needs a professional? Put the dates in your calendar. A calendar beats a memory.

Gardening is a quiet AI win. Take a photo of a plant. Ask what it is and what it needs. Ask when to water it. Ask why the leaves are yellow. Ask for a planting plan for your soil and sunlight. AI knows a lot about plants. It still cannot feel your soil. Check its advice against a local guide. Local weather and soil matter more than general rules.

Decorating and organising are about decisions. Ask AI for colour ideas for a room. Describe the light, the size, and the furniture. Ask for three palettes. Ask for a layout plan. Ask for storage ideas for a small space. Then test with samples. A colour on a screen is not a colour on a wall.

Moving house is a project. Ask AI for a checklist by week. What to book. What to pack first. What to label. Ask for a box-labelling system. Ask for a first-night box list. The first-night box holds the kettle, the phone chargers, the bedding, and the toilet paper. Pack it last. Open it first.

Pets count too. Ask AI for feeding guidance by breed and weight. Ask for exercise ideas. Ask what plants are toxic to cats or dogs. Then check with your vet. AI is a starting point for pet care, not the final word.

## The AI-powered smart home

A smart home is a home where devices talk to each other. Lights. Heating. Speakers. Locks. AI makes them easier to use. You can ask for routines. "At seven, turn the heating on and play the news." You can ask for automations. "When I leave, turn everything off." You can ask for help setting them up.

Keep it simple. One routine that works beats ten that glitch. Start with heating and lights. Add more only when the first ones work. And keep security in mind. A smart lock needs a strong password and updates. A camera needs a safe place to store its video. Read the privacy settings. Your home is private. Keep it that way.

## Check yourself

1. What is the rule for AI advice in the home?
**Answer:** AI advises and you decide, checking recipes by tasting, repairs by looking, and plans by measuring.

2. How do you use AI safely for a recipe?
**Answer:** List what you have, state allergies and numbers, check quantities and allergens, and taste as you go.

3. Which DIY jobs should beginners not attempt?
**Answer:** Gas, mains electricity, structural walls, and roofs, where you should call a professional instead.

4. What is a first-night box when moving house?
**Answer:** A box packed last and opened first, holding the kettle, chargers, bedding, and toilet paper for the first night.

5. Why start a smart home with just one routine?
**Answer:** Because one routine that works beats ten that glitch, and you can add more only once the first ones work.

## In short

- AI is a pocket chef, a fix-it guide, and a project planner for your home.
- Cook from your fridge, plan the week's meals, and check every recipe for allergens.
- AI can teach safe repairs, but gas, electricity, and structure need a professional.
- A yearly maintenance calendar and a planting plan save money and stress.
- Start a smart home with one routine that works, and keep security tight.
